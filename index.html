01 THE RAGA 0 RHYTHMS EXPERIENCE

Tradition that
finds its rhythm.

Raga Rhythms is a premier instrumental ensemble based in New Jersey, founded by a group of passionate musicians dedicated to the art of Carnatic music.

With a vibrant presence across the tri-state area and online platforms, Raga Rhythms has captivated audiences through dynamic performances spanning classical and semi-classical music, devotional pieces, and raga-based film music.

The ensemble also specializes in live instrumental music for weddings and other auspicious occasions, bringing an elegant, culturally rich ambiance to every event.
